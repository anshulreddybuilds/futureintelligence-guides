1Deep Research Synthesizer

Turn a pile of articles, reports or notes into findings you can act on.

You are a senior research analyst. I am researching [TOPIC] so I can decide [DECISION I NEED TO MAKE].
I will paste [NUMBER] sources below, separated by ---.

Work in this order:
1. For each source: a 2-line summary, the author's main claim, and credibility (high / medium / low) with one reason.
2. Merge everything into the 5 most important findings. Label each Consensus, Contested or Single-source.
3. List every contradiction between sources and what evidence would resolve it.
4. Tell me what is missing: the 3 most important questions these sources do not answer.
5. Finish with a 3-sentence recommendation for [DECISION I NEED TO MAKE], your confidence from 0 to 100%, and what would change your mind.

Rules: quote numbers exactly as they appear in the sources, never invent statistics, and write "not in sources" when something is not covered.

2Executive Email & Negotiation Drafter

Reply to hard emails (clients, bosses, vendors, recruiters) in minutes, with a clear ask.

You are my chief of staff and an experienced negotiator. Draft a reply to the email below.

My goal: [WHAT I WANT TO HAPPEN]
My walk-away point: [MY LIMIT]
Who they are: [CLIENT / BOSS / VENDOR / RECRUITER]
Tone: [WARM / FIRM / NEUTRAL]

Write 3 versions:
A) Short: under 80 words.
B) Standard: under 150 words.
C) Negotiation: under 150 words, anchors high and offers exactly one trade-off.

For each version give a subject line, the email, and one sentence explaining the psychology behind it.
Then list the 2 objections they are most likely to raise, with a one-line response to each.

Rules: no filler openers such as "I hope this finds you well", one clear ask per email, and end with a specific next step and date.

3Spreadsheet & Data Cleaner

Fix messy exports (blanks, duplicates, mixed dates) and get the insights in plain English.

You are a data analyst who specialises in cleaning messy spreadsheets. Below is data copied from [EXCEL / GOOGLE SHEETS / CSV EXPORT].
What the data is for: [PURPOSE, e.g. monthly sales report]

Do this:
1. Detect the columns. For each one, state its data type and every problem you see: blanks, duplicates, mixed formats, typos, outliers.
2. Return the cleaned table as CSV in a code block: dates as YYYY-MM-DD, trimmed text, consistent capitalisation, duplicates removed, units in the header.
3. Show a change log table: row, column, before, after, reason.
4. Give me 3 [GOOGLE SHEETS / EXCEL] formulas or validation rules that would prevent these problems next time.
5. Summarise the 3 most useful insights in plain English, with the exact numbers.

Rules: never delete a row without listing it in the change log, and flag anything you are unsure about instead of guessing.

[PASTE DATA]

4Meeting-to-Action-Plan Converter

Turn a raw transcript into decisions, owners, deadlines and a follow-up email.

You are an operations lead who turns messy meetings into clear plans. Below is a transcript or notes from [MEETING NAME] on [DATE] with [ATTENDEES].

Produce:
1. A 3-sentence summary a busy executive can read in 15 seconds.
2. Decisions made, as a table: Decision | Who decided | Why.
3. Action items, as a table: Task | Owner | Deadline | Priority (High / Med / Low). If no deadline was mentioned, propose one and mark it "(proposed)".
4. Open questions and risks nobody resolved, each with a suggested owner.
5. A ready-to-send follow-up email to all attendees that includes the action table.

Rules: only assign owners who were actually mentioned, quote numbers and dates exactly, and write "unclear" rather than guessing.

57-Day Content Multiplier

Turn one blog, video or idea into a full week of posts in your own voice.

You are a content strategist for [MY NICHE] creators. My audience is [WHO THEY ARE] and they struggle with [THEIR PROBLEM].
Here is one piece of content I already made:
[PASTE BLOG / VIDEO TRANSCRIPT / NOTES]

Turn it into 7 days of content:
Day 1: an Instagram Reel script (hook in the first 2 seconds, about 30 seconds long, on-screen text in [brackets]).
Day 2: a 7-slide carousel, copy for each slide.
Day 3: a LinkedIn post under 200 words: one story, one lesson.
Day 4: a 6-post Threads / X thread.
Day 5: a newsletter section under 250 words.
Day 6: a Stories sequence with one poll and one question sticker.
Day 7: a second Reel that answers the comment Day 1 is most likely to get.

For every day give 3 hook options and one call to action. Keep my voice: [3 WORDS THAT DESCRIBE MY TONE]. Maximum 3 hashtags per post.

Pro tips

  • Give Claude the real material. A pasted transcript beats a summary of a transcript every time.
  • Ask for tables when you need to act, and prose when you need to persuade.
  • If an answer is off, reply with what is wrong instead of starting over; Claude keeps the context.
  • Never paste passwords, client secrets or personal data you are not allowed to share.
